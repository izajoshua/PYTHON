Telecom Customer Churn Analysis – Project Overview

This project explores customer churn behavior using a detailed telecom dataset of 7,043 customers. The analysis focuses on identifying high-risk customer segments, uncovering usage patterns, and highlighting key factors that drive churn. Using exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualizations, and statistical insights, the project provides a clear understanding of customer behavior and actionable recommendations for retention strategies.

🔍 What This Project Covers

Comprehensive demographic and service-usage analysis

Tenure-based loyalty patterns

Internet service impact, including high-risk fiber-optic users

Contract, billing, and payment method influence on churn

Financial analysis of monthly and total charges

Identification of critical churn drivers

Data-driven retention strategies

📈 Key Insights

Senior citizens, fiber-optic users, and electronic check payers show the highest churn.

Month-to-month contracts remain the most volatile customer segment with ~43% churn.

Value-added services (security, backup, tech support) significantly reduce churn.

The first 6 months of onboarding are crucial in customer retention.

Long-tenure customers and those with long-term contracts show strong loyalty.

🎯 Outcome

This analysis helps telecom businesses identify churn-prone customer groups, optimize pricing and service bundles, strengthen onboarding strategies, and improve overall customer retention.
